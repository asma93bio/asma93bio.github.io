Research

In Silico Evaluation of Asparagus racemosus Saponins and Phytoestrogens as Estrogen Receptor Alpha Inhibitors in Breast Cancer
M.S. thesis — targets: ERα, mTOR, MEK — defended with Honors (Mention Excellent)

Evaluated four bioactive compounds (Shatavarin IV, Shatavaroside A, Shatavarin VIII, Shatavarin IX) via molecular docking against ERα, mTOR, and MEK. Results showed strong competition with endogenous estradiol and multi-pathway disruption, with Shatavaroside A exhibiting the highest target affinity — supporting Shatavari-based therapies as multi-target alternatives to single-pathway endocrine treatment.
